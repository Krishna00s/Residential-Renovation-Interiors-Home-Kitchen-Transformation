\# SITE ARCHITECTURE

\## Premium Home Renovation / Construction Website



\---



\## 01. ARCHITECTURAL PHILOSOPHY



This website must NOT feel like a single long landing page.



It should feel like a complete, premium digital experience for an established home renovation and construction company.



The website should have multiple dedicated pages with clear navigation, meaningful page transitions, interactive sections and a consistent visual language.



The goal is to create a website that could realistically be presented to a real renovation / construction company as a premium website concept.



The architecture must remain practical enough to implement entirely on the frontend.



No backend functionality is required.



The experience should prioritize:



\- Visual storytelling

\- Strong typography

\- High-quality imagery

\- Reactive scrolling

\- Smooth page transitions

\- Interactive project presentation

\- Clear service organization

\- Strong conversion points

\- Premium editorial layouts

\- Mobile responsiveness

\- Performance-conscious animations



Do NOT turn every section into a card grid.



Do NOT make every page structurally identical.



Each page should have its own visual rhythm while still belonging to the same design system.



\---



\# 02. GLOBAL NAVIGATION



The primary navigation should contain:



\- Home

\- Services

\- Projects

\- Process

\- About

\- Contact



Primary CTA:



\- Get a Quote



The navigation should feel minimal and premium.



It should not consume excessive screen space.



Possible behavior:



\- Transparent navigation over the hero

\- Changes appearance depending on background contrast

\- Becomes slightly more compact while scrolling

\- Smoothly hides or transforms when appropriate

\- Reappears naturally when the user scrolls upward



Avoid conventional bulky navigation bars.



\---



\# 03. HOME PAGE



Route:



`/`



Purpose:



The Home page is the primary storytelling experience.



It should immediately communicate:



1\. What the company does

2\. The quality of its work

3\. Its design philosophy

4\. The breadth of its services

5\. Its process

6\. Its strongest projects

7\. Why a customer should contact them



The Home page should NOT attempt to explain everything.



It should create curiosity and guide users deeper into the website.



\---



\## HOME — SECTION 01

\### Cinematic Hero



The hero should be visually dominant.



Do NOT place a static rectangular image on one half of the screen.



Instead:



\- Use imagery as part of the entire visual composition

\- Allow imagery to extend behind typography

\- Use overlays, gradients, blur and controlled contrast where necessary

\- Typography should interact visually with the image

\- Image movement should respond subtly to scrolling

\- The hero should feel like an editorial composition rather than a traditional website hero



Possible content:



Large statement:



"We don't just renovate homes.

We reshape how you live."



Supporting copy should be short.



Primary CTA:



"Start Your Project"



Secondary CTA:



"Explore Our Work"



Optional cinematic interaction:



"Watch Our Story"



The hero should establish the premium visual language immediately.



\---



\## HOME — SECTION 02

\### What We Do



Introduce the company through a strong statement rather than a generic heading.



Example direction:



"Thoughtful renovation.

Built around the way you live."



Present the major service categories.



Services should visually interact with the page.



Potential service categories:



\- Kitchen Renovations

\- Bathroom Renovations

\- Home Extensions

\- Attic / Loft Conversions

\- Full Home Renovations

\- Energy Upgrades

\- Interior Transformations



These should NOT simply appear as seven identical cards.



Use an interactive composition such as:



\- Expanding image panels

\- Horizontal scrolling gallery

\- Cursor/hover-driven previews

\- Sticky text with changing imagery

\- Scroll-driven service transitions



Each service should link to the dedicated Services page or relevant service detail.



\---



\## HOME — SECTION 03

\### The Transformation



Show the idea of transformation.



This section should visually communicate:



BEFORE → DESIGN → AFTER



Possible interaction:



As the user scrolls:



1\. Original space appears

2\. Design concept overlays or replaces it

3\. Finished space is revealed



Use motion and image transitions instead of conventional comparison cards.



This section is important because it immediately demonstrates the value of the business.



\---



\## HOME — SECTION 04

\### Our Process



Introduce the company's process.



Suggested stages:



01 — Consultation

02 — Design

03 — Planning

04 — Build

05 — Handover



The section should use reactive scrolling.



As the user scrolls:



\- The current stage becomes active

\- Supporting imagery changes

\- Typography changes subtly

\- Progress indicator moves

\- Previous stages become visually quieter



Do not use a basic horizontal timeline.



The process should feel cinematic and interactive.

\# 13. PAGE TRANSITIONS



Page navigation should feel connected.



When navigating between pages:



\- Avoid harsh instant page changes

\- Use smooth transitions

\- Preserve visual continuity

\- Use imagery or typography as transition elements where appropriate



Transitions must remain performant.



Do not use excessive animation simply for the sake of animation.



\---



\# 14. RESPONSIVE ARCHITECTURE



The architecture must work across:



\- Desktop

\- Laptop

\- Tablet

\- Mobile



Desktop can contain the most advanced interactions.



On mobile:



\- Preserve the storytelling

\- Simplify complex interactions

\- Convert horizontal interactions into vertical experiences where necessary

\- Maintain strong typography

\- Maintain image quality

\- Avoid oversized animations that hurt performance



Do not simply shrink the desktop design.



Mobile should have its own thoughtful composition.



\---



\# 15. FRONTEND-ONLY REQUIREMENT



This entire demo is frontend-focused.



Use realistic mock data where necessary.



No backend is required for:



\- Contact form

\- Quote form

\- Project filtering

\- Service filtering

\- Project data

\- Journal content



Use local/static data structures.



The architecture should make it easy to replace mock content with real backend functionality later.

\# 18. CONTENT STRATEGY



The website should communicate through a combination of:



\- Typography

\- Photography

\- Motion

\- Short copy

\- Numbers

\- Project details

\- Interactive elements



Avoid huge paragraphs.



Avoid generic marketing language.



Avoid repetitive "We provide high-quality..." copy.



The writing should feel confident, specific and human.



\---



\# 19. INTERACTION PRIORITY



The most important interactive experiences should be:



1\. Hero scroll interaction

2\. Service exploration

3\. Before/after transformation

4\. Process storytelling

5\. Project exploration

6\. Project detail storytelling

7\. Page transitions

8\. CTA transitions



These interactions should receive more attention than decorative animations.



The website should feel responsive to the user's movement.



Scrolling should feel like the user is progressing through a story.



\---



\# 20. FINAL EXPERIENCE GOAL



When someone visits this website, the experience should communicate:



"This company takes its work seriously."



It should feel:



\- Premium

\- Modern

\- Crafted

\- Trustworthy

\- Creative

\- Human

\- Interactive

\- Memorable



The website should be visually impressive enough to attract attention while remaining realistic enough that a real renovation company could actually use the design.



The website is ultimately a sales demonstration.



Every major visual decision should support that goal.
