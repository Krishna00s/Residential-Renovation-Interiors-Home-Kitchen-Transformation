\# Premium Home Renovation / Construction Website



\---



\# 01 — PURPOSE



This document defines the content model and demonstration content for the website.



The website must feel like a real, established Irish residential renovation and home transformation company.



Content should support the visual storytelling defined in:



\- 01-VISION.md

\- 02-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md

\- 03-SITE-ARCHITECTURE.md

\- 04-INTERACTION-MOTION.md



Content must never fight the design.



The website should use concise, confident and human language.



Avoid long blocks of marketing copy.



Avoid generic AI-generated business language.



Avoid repetitive statements such as:



"We provide high-quality services to our customers."



Every piece of content should communicate something useful.

# 33 — CONTENT / DESIGN RELATIONSHIP

Content should never dictate a generic layout.

Instead:

Content should be edited to support the intended composition.

If a section needs one sentence:

Use one sentence.

If a project needs a single powerful statement:

Use one statement.

Do not fill empty space with unnecessary copy.

Whitespace is intentional.

Typography should remain the primary communication tool.

Images should provide emotional context.

Motion should help guide attention rather than compensate for weak content.

---

# 34 — FINAL CONTENT QUALITY BAR

Before content is approved, ask:

Does it sound like a real person?

Is it specific?

Does it communicate something useful?

Is it concise?

Does it support the visual composition?

Does it avoid generic AI language?

Does it avoid exaggerated claims?

Would a real homeowner understand it immediately?

Would the content still sound natural if spoken by a real company representative?

If not:

Rewrite it.

---

# 35 — FINAL PRINCIPLE

The website should never feel like:

"Here are our services."

It should feel like:

"Here is what we believe about homes.

Here is what we have created.

Here is how we work.

Here is what your transformation could look like."

The content exists to support the experience.

The photography creates emotion.

The typography creates hierarchy.

The motion creates continuity.

The content creates trust.

The final combination should create desire to begin a conversation.
